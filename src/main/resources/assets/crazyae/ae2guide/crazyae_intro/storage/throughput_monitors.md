# Throughput monitors

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="throughput_storage_monitor" scale="4" />
  <ItemImage id="throughput_conversion_monitor" scale="4" />
</Row>

[Storage monitors](ae2:items-blocks-machines/monitors.md) that show not only how much of an item you have,
but also **how fast the amount changes** — great for checking a farm or an ore processing line.

- Right-click with an item to choose what to watch, just like the AE2 monitor.
- The conversion version also lets you put items in and take them out by clicking, like the AE2 conversion monitor.
