## Energy

Portable cells run on AE energy. Charge them in an <ItemLink id="ae2:charger" /> or take a charged one from the creative tab.
<ItemLink id="ae2:energy_card" /> in the cell increase its battery.

## Auto pickup (item cells)

- **Shift + right-click** with the cell toggles auto pickup.
- While it is on, items you pick up go straight into the cell (if it is somewhere in your inventory and has power).
- Partition the cell in a <ItemLink id="ae2:cell_workbench" /> to collect only what you want, for example only ores.

## Recipe

The cell is crafted from a storage component of the same size, a cell housing, an ME chest and a dense energy cell.
