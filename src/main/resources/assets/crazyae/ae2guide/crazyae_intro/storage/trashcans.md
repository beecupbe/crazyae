# Trash cans

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="trashcan_items" scale="4" />
  <BlockImage id="trashcan_fluids" scale="4" />
</Row>

A network device that **destroys** what you tell it to. Put items (or fluids) into its **72 filter slots**,
and the trash can keeps pulling them out of ME storage and deleting them.

- Perfect for cobblestone, dirt, rotten flesh and other junk that clogs storage.
- Without upgrades it deletes 32 items or buckets per tick. Each <ItemLink id="stacks_card" /> multiplies that by 4 (up to 4 cards).
- **Careful:** it deletes everything that matches, including items you might still need. Double-check the filter.
