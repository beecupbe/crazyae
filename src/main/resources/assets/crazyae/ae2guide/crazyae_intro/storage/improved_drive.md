# ME Improved Drive

<BlockImage id="improved_drive" scale="4" />

An [ME Drive](ae2:items-blocks-machines/drive.md) with **35 cell slots** instead of 10 — and still one channel.
Works with any cells: AE2, Crazy AE and other addons.

The side button sets the [priority](ae2:ae2-mechanics/import-export-storage.md) of the drive.
