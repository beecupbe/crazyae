[Energy cells](ae2:items-blocks-machines/energy_cells.md) that store far more AE. They buffer power for the network
and smooth out spikes, for example when a huge crafting job starts.

| Cell | Capacity (default) |
|------|--------------------|
| <ItemLink id="improved_energy_cell" /> | 16 777 216 AE |
| <ItemLink id="advanced_energy_cell" /> | 134 217 728 AE |
| <ItemLink id="perfect_energy_cell" /> | 805 306 368 AE |
| <ItemLink id="quantum_energy_cell" /> | 17 179 869 184 AE |

Capacities can be changed in the config. A broken cell keeps its charge, and the creative tab has both empty and fully charged cells.
