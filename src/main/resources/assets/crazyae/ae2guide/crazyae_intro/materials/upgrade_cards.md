# Upgrade cards

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="improved_speed_card" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="advanced_speed_card" scale="3" />
  <ItemImage id="stacks_card" scale="3" />
</Row>

Crazy AE machines use their own [upgrade cards](ae2:items-blocks-machines/upgrade_cards.md) in addition to the AE2 ones.

- <ItemLink id="improved_speed_card" /> — a stronger acceleration card; several can be installed.
- <ItemLink id="advanced_speed_card" /> — the strongest acceleration card, only one per device.
- <ItemLink id="stacks_card" /> — the device handles more items per operation (whole stacks instead of single items).

## Where they fit

Numbers are the maximum cards per device.

| Device | <ItemLink id="improved_speed_card" /> | <ItemLink id="advanced_speed_card" /> | <ItemLink id="stacks_card" /> |
|--------|------|------|------|
| <ItemLink id="improved_mac" /> | — | — | 5 |
| <ItemLink id="improved_io_port" /> | 3 | 1 | 3 |
| <ItemLink id="improved_crystal_charger" /> | 5 | 1 | 5 |
| <ItemLink id="improved_import_bus" />, <ItemLink id="improved_export_bus" /> | 4 | 1 | 4 |
| <ItemLink id="trashcan_items" />, <ItemLink id="trashcan_fluids" /> | — | — | 4 |

The tooltip of a card also lists the devices that accept it.
