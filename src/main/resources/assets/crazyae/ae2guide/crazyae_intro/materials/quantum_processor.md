# Quantum Processor

<ItemImage id="quantum_processor" scale="4" />

The main component of Crazy AE: it is used in most machines and cells of the addon.
It is pressed in an <ItemLink id="ae2:inscriber" /> from AE2 [processors](ae2:items-blocks-machines/processors.md) and a matter ball.

Do not confuse it with the [ME Quantum CPU](../autocrafting/quantum_cpu.md) — that is a crafting block.
