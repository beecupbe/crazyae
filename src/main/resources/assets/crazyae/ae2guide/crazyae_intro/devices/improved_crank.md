# Improved Wooden Crank

<BlockImage id="improved_crank" scale="4" />

A [crank](ae2:items-blocks-machines/crank.md) that gives **two turns per click** instead of one.
Put it on a <ItemLink id="ae2:charger" /> to charge crystals by hand twice as fast before you have power.
