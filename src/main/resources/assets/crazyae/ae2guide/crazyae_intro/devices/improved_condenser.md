# Improved Condenser

<BlockImage id="improved_condenser" scale="4" />

A [Matter Condenser](ae2:items-blocks-machines/condenser.md) with **25 input slots** and **4 output slots**.
It destroys anything you put in and turns it into energy, matter balls or singularities.

- Accepts Crazy AE storage components in the component slot, so it can hold far more energy before producing a singularity.
- Its storage is **8 times** larger than the component's capacity.
