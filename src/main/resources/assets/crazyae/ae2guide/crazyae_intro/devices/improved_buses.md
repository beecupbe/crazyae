# Improved import and export buses

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="improved_import_bus" scale="4" />
  <ItemImage id="improved_export_bus" scale="4" />
</Row>

Faster versions of the [import bus](ae2:items-blocks-machines/import_bus.md) and the [export bus](ae2:items-blocks-machines/export_bus.md).
They move **256 items per operation** out of the box and accept extra upgrades:

| Upgrade | Max |
|---------|-----|
| <ItemLink id="improved_speed_card" /> | 4 |
| <ItemLink id="advanced_speed_card" /> | 1 |
| <ItemLink id="stacks_card" /> | 4 |
| <ItemLink id="ae2:capacity_card" /> | 2 |
| <ItemLink id="ae2:fuzzy_card" />, <ItemLink id="ae2:redstone_card" /> | 1 |
| <ItemLink id="ae2:crafting_card" /> (export only) | 1 |
| <ItemLink id="ae2:inverter_card" /> (import only) | 1 |
