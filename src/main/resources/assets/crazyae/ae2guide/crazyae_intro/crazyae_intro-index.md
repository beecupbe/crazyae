Crazy AE is an addon for Applied Energistics 2 for those who have outgrown vanilla AE2 numbers.
It adds huge storage cells, compact crafting CPUs, fast machines and a lot of power.

Pick a section below. Every page shows the recipe and what the block or item is good for.
Hold **G** over any Crazy AE item in your inventory or JEI to jump straight to its page.

## Where to start

1. Craft a <ItemLink id="quantum_processor" /> in an <ItemLink id="ae2:inscriber" />: it is used in most recipes of the addon.
2. Upgrade your storage with [bigger cells](storage/storage_cells.md) and the [Improved Drive](storage/improved_drive.md).
3. Replace multiblock crafting CPUs with a [Crafting Units Combiner](autocrafting/crafting_units_combiner.md)
   and, later, with the [ME Quantum CPU](autocrafting/quantum_cpu.md).
